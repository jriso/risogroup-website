Which BI tools document where AI data goes?

5 of 15 tools do, according to their public docs, and 9 partly.

Yes: Databricks, BigQuery, Tableau, Alteryx, Mode. Partly: Snowflake, Omni, Hex, Sigma, Looker, Power BI, Metabase, MotherDuck, Redshift.

The question we asked: Does the product documentation say which services receive AI prompts or data, and what they receive?

Grading standard

yes: names the services that receive AI prompts or data, what they receive, and how long it is kept. Missing any one of the three is partly. A subprocessor list is not required; one published only on a separate trust or legal site is outside this documentation, so its absence here is nothing found, not no.
