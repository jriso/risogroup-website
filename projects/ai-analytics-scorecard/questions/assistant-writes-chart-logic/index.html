Which BI tools' AI can write chart logic?

10 of 15 tools do, according to their public docs, and 4 partly.

Yes: Databricks, Snowflake, Omni, Hex, Sigma, Looker, Tableau, Power BI, Metabase, MotherDuck. Partly: BigQuery, Alteryx, Mode, Redshift.
