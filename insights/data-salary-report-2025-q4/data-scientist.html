Senior vs. Non-Senior

The seniority premium for Data Scientists is significant, with Senior Data Scientists earning approximately 52% more at the median level. The gap widens further at higher percentiles, reflecting the premium placed on experienced practitioners who can lead projects and mentor teams.

Key Observations

  • The median Senior Data Scientist earns $175k, compared to $115k for non-senior roles - a 52% premium.
  • P95 compensation reaches $265k for seniors, often at companies with strong ML/AI focus or in high cost-of-living markets.
  • Entry-level roles (P25) start around $95k, making Data Science accessible with the right skills and training.
  • The interquartile range is wider for seniors, reflecting more variation in responsibilities and company compensation strategies.
