Q4 2025 Compensation Data

Key Findings

  • AI Engineers command the highest compensation, with median salaries 30% above Data Scientists and 85% above Data Analysts.
  • Senior premiums vary significantly by role: ML Engineers see the largest jump (~28%) while Data Analysts see smaller gains (~25%).
  • The P95 ceiling for AI Engineers exceeds $300k, reflecting intense competition for top talent in generative AI.
